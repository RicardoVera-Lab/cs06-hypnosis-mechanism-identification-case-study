# Claims and Limitations

## Supported public claims

CIEC LAB can document that this case produced:

- a structured rival-mechanism research framework;
- explicit handling of measurement error and non-identifiability;
- computational model-recovery and confusion testing;
- explicit abstention behavior when evidence cannot discriminate;
- an audit of secondary-data suitability;
- source-separated calibration without pseudo-pooling;
- reproducible final computational outputs;
- a formal research closure rather than an indefinite research loop.

## Claims not supported by this case

This case does not establish:

- the true mechanism of hypnosis;
- clinical efficacy;
- superiority of any induction;
- a universal neurological “window” after surprise;
- patient selection rules;
- safety incidence;
- therapeutic benefit;
- novelty or priority.

## Why the limitations matter

A high-reliability research system is not measured only by how often it produces an exciting positive result.

It is also measured by whether it can distinguish what survived, what failed, what remains uncertain, and what evidence would actually settle the issue.

That behavior is the central public result of CS06.
