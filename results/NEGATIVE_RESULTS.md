# Negative Results That Increased the Value of the Case

High-reliability research should preserve what failed.

## Strong surprise-specific mechanism claim

Not supported as a demonstrated general explanation.

## Universal short “window”

Not supported as a general scientific rule by this case.

## “Critical filter” shutdown story

Not established as a literal mechanism.

## Hypnosis-specific “cognitive vacuum”

Not established.

## First-suggestion privilege

Not established as a universal mechanism.

## Unitary arousal explanation

Not retained as a single mechanism; physiological activation had to remain componentized.

## Why this is commercially relevant

An R&D system that kills weak explanations early protects capital.

A false positive that survives into product, clinical, legal, investment or strategy decisions can be dramatically more expensive than a negative result discovered during research.

CS06 demonstrates that CIEC LAB can preserve value even when the starting claim does not survive intact.
