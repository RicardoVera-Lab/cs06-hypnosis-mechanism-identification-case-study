# Scientific Closure

## What survived

### Expectation / context / demand family

Computationally distinguishable in principle under sufficiently precise measurement.

A central warning emerged: measurement error in expectation can create a false residual that resembles a separate contextual pathway.

### Attention / predictive-updating family

Partially distinguishable.

Under poorer parameter stability, the region in which the correct scientific action is to abstain expands substantially.

The failure of one proxy does not validate its rival.

## What existing evidence could not do

The audited secondary evidence did not contain the complete variable structure needed to decisively adjudicate the surviving families.

Four sources were useful for bounded calibration.

None was elevated into a decisive dataset.

## Final conclusion

The case produced a disciplined map of what can be separated, what cannot yet be separated, what measurement quality is required, what evidence would change the decision, and where further work becomes a genuinely new research project.

That is the closed deliverable of CS06.
